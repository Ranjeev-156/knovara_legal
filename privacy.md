# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

## What we collect
- Email address and name (via Supabase Auth)
- Encrypted API keys if you use the BYOK feature
- Documents you upload to your knowledge workspace
- Chat messages you send to the assistant

## How we use it
- Email and name are used for authentication only.
- Uploaded documents are stored in your private workspace and never shared.
- API keys are encrypted at rest with AES-128 and are never exposed to other users.
- Chat messages are stored so you can access your conversation history.

## What we don't do
- We do not sell your data.
- We do not share your data with third parties except the AI providers you explicitly choose.

## Data deletion
You can delete your documents, chats, and API keys at any time from within the app.
